LinkedIn Webhook Exporter

Everything here is stored only in this browser profile. Nothing is sent anywhere except to the webhook URL below.

Destination

Must be https://. http://localhost is allowed for development.
Standard Webhooks secrets are base64 and usually start with whsec_. Receivers verify with the same secret. Every request also carries Idempotency-Key.

Payload

Safety

Bulk export

LinkedIn shows at most 100 pages × 25 = 2,500 results per search. Split larger searches with filters. Exports also respect the daily cap below.
LinkedIn restricts accounts that capture too aggressively. Community guidance is 50 to 100 profiles per day per account. The default cap is 100. Bulk capture from Sales Navigator search pages is the most-restricted pattern.

Activity log

Every capture, send, retry, export step, settings change and error is recorded locally (last 1,000 entries, no secrets). Nothing is uploaded.
