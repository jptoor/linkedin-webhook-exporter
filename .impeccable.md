# Design context

## Who
Sales reps and SDRs working inside LinkedIn Sales Navigator all day. They are not
engineers. They already use lemlist, Frontier (Exportly), ZoomInfo and similar
side panels and expect the same shape: a panel that shows what they are looking
at, lets them pick people, and pushes them somewhere with one button.

## Jobs
1. Push the person I am looking at into a play/campaign.
2. Pick a handful of people across several result pages, then push them all.
3. Hand a whole Sales Navigator search to the backend and let it fetch the
   members ("Import this search"). Never page through results in the browser.
4. See that it worked (or why not) without reading a log.

## Tone
Quiet, confident, fast. Plain sales vocabulary: push, selected, search import,
play. Never: webhook, payload, basket, capture, signing secret, schema (those
live behind "Advanced" on the options page). One primary action per screen,
pinned at the bottom like lemlist's "Push all to campaign".

## Visual identity
- Brand: Deepline. Mark and wordmark in `src/brand/`. Ink: `#1C112A` (deep plum),
  the only accent. Paper: warm off-white tinted toward the plum, never pure white.
- Type: system UI stack (the extension ships no web fonts by design; it must work
  offline and under LinkedIn's CSP). Hierarchy comes from weight and size, not
  from a display face.
- No gradients, no glass, no glows, no icon-grid cards, no purple CTAs other than
  the brand ink. Rounded but restrained (8px). Motion only for state changes.
- The one memorable thing: the pinned action bar that always says exactly what
  will happen, e.g. "Push 4 people to Warm intro".
